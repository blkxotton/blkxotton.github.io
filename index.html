Our Foundation

More Than
a Garment.

Cotton carries a history that reaches far beyond fabric. Its story is tied to land, labor, ancestry, survival, innovation, and the shaping of culture.

BLK XOTTON uses clothing as a doorway into that history. Every piece is designed to invite reflection, preserve knowledge, and connect the past to the present.

01

History

Understanding cotton's role in the movement, labor, and experiences of generations.

Culture

Honoring the people, creativity, and identity woven through the story of the material.

03

Connection

Creating garments that connect what we wear with what we know and remember.

The Experience

The Story Travels
With the Shirt.

Each garment will include a scannable code that opens a deeper educational experience. Wearers will be able to explore the history of cotton, its cultural significance, its relationship to the body, and the stories connected to every collection.

Scan. Learn. Carry It Forward.
